My network names

Your SSIDs (for example foofoo1, foofoo2). A device that probes for one of these is treated as yours. Add the name exactly as it appears.

My networks

Access points and hotspots that are yours. The device is marked known, and any Wi-Fi client connected to it is marked known automatically and never counted as a rotating address. Add every BSSID your equipment broadcasts (one per SSID and band on many routers).

Vehicles

Tyre-pressure sensors heard by the SDR. A car has up to four. They transmit while the wheels turn, so this shows arrivals and departures. Mark your own vehicles known so they stop alerting.

Reset

Deletes every discovered device, sighting, vehicle, entity and alert, and restarts the learning period. Your My networks list and network names are kept unless you choose to remove them too.

Suggested links

Records that may belong to the same person, vehicle or household, or that match an entity you already made. The evidence is probed network names (compared against everything an entity has shown across all its devices), tyre sensors heard together, and devices that appear in the same time windows. An entity does not need every signal on every visit. Suggestions are evidence, not proof, and nothing links until you accept it.
